# ENDES2024: a defensible later-year replication with explicit limits

20 October2026. Source, measurement and preparation review; this document contains no exposure-effect estimate or prediction result. Existing2023 results and manuscript files are preserved.

**The public release supports a fixed retrospective train2023/test2024 prediction comparison in observed complete linked women, with a separate new-PSU sensitivity. It does not establish national joint-sample performance, person follow-up, causal effects, pandemic effects or prospective confirmation.** The later outcomes have not been used to select algorithms or hypotheses in this source-readiness stage.

## Access and provenance

The official [Peruvian public-data catalogue](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/dataset/encuesta-demogr%C3%A1fica-y-de-salud-familiar-endes-2024-instituto-nacional-de-estad%C3%ADstica-e) identifies INEI as the producer, explicitly lists Public access and the Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL), and links the [complete2024 archive](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/2024.zip). The downloaded catalogue HTML, three dictionaries, interviewer manual and technical sheet are retained with retrieval URLs and SHA256 hashes. The complete archive is71,056,297bytes, SHA256`832fd314e4ef94796cb5b24000dc25884fbeaf5a15b6fccc9d2701d83d63913f`. Its module ZIPs contain the CSVs and additional source dictionaries/questionnaires. `MODULE_LOCATIONS.json` and `SOURCE_INVENTORY.json` identify the exact outer/inner members; both ZIP levels pass CRC checks.

Archive, linked respondents, source keys and person-level eligibility flags stay exclusively in excluded `data_local/`. Public packaging must omit that directory even though the acquisition itself is authorized and public. This preserves the repository's respondent-data handling convention; public accessibility alone is not used to invent an ethics approval or formal exemption.

## Measurements and selection

The [2024 individual dictionary](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Dicionario_BD_Individual_ENDES_2024.pdf#page=89) documents the same five justification scenarios: going out without telling the husband, neglecting children, arguing, refusing sex and burning food. `V744A`–`V744E` use0=no,1=yes,8=unknown. Unknowns are excluded rather than recoded no. The sum0–5 and fixed scenario contrasts preserve the original question distinction between overall endorsement and scenario pattern. Responses concern endorsement; they are not experienced IPV.

The [2024 health dictionary, PDFpage10](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Dicionario_BD_Salud_ENDES_2024.pdf#page=10) and [interviewer manual, PDFpage367](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2024-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=367) identify the actual nine`QS700A`–`QS700I` PHQ-9 items and14-day recall. Codes0–3 are retained and summed to0–27, with complete responses required. Released frequency labels are0=no days,1=1–6days,2=7–11days,3=12or more days. Their nonstandard item order does not change the sum. The separate recalled-episode`QS704*` block is not substituted. These are symptom scores, not diagnoses. Source-compatible scoring is supported; psychometric measurement invariance across years has not been established by this audit.

The [2024 individual dictionary, PDFpages101–102](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/Dicionario_BD_Individual_ENDES_2024.pdf#page=101) retains three observed emotional acts`D103A/B/D`, seven physical acts`D105A`–`D105G` and two sexual acts`D105H/I`. The [manual, PDFpages317–318](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2024-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=317) distinguishes whether acts ever occurred from frequency during the preceding12months. Recent exposure requires any1/2; lifetime exposure includes1/2/3. Code3 is never interpreted as no lifetime experience. Required items must all have valid0–3 responses. `D103C` is completely blank in the actual2024 release, as in2023; it is not imputed, and the three-act emotional block is not called exhaustive psychological abuse. Religion`V130` is also completely blank in both releases, so religion adjustment cannot be performed.

The [manual, PDFpage315](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2024-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=315) describes selected-woman DV interviews and privacy requirements. [PDFpage330](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2024-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=330) separately selects one health respondent aged15+ using the most recent birthday method. Preparation therefore repeats the exact previously fixed observed-intersection rules: women18–49; completed individual interview; unique female18–49 same-person health link; both health completion flags; selected and interviewed DV/private interview; ever married/cohabiting; complete required items; valid native baseline categories; agreeing PSU/stratum/age fields. Household/person-line links are one-to-one, attitudes and DV link on unique CASEID, and household attributes link many-to-one. No name-based linkage is used. Sequential exclusions, unknowns, native codes and unmatched joins are exported as aggregate ledgers.

Age, education, relative wealth quintile, current work, marital status, residence and departmental region retain their source-native definitions. Current work is not silently changed to past-year employment; marital and education categories are not equated with another survey or the legacy aggregate table.

## Actual time and independence gates

The actual2024 individual module has37,117records, all`V007=2024`; the selected-health module has34,018records, all`QSINTY=2024`. The health dictionary's allowed2023–2024year range is not an observed sample description. The individual dictionary retains a stale pre2024`V007` range; source-record years and individual interviewCMC are checked directly instead of treating that stale printed range as the truth. Every eligible later-test record has individual and health interview dates in2024.

The frozen2023 prepared cohort includes10,267women, of whom10,259have both actual interviews in2023 and8have both in2024. This new temporal analysis must exclude those8from training. The original2023-release analysis remains unchanged. The date correction was discovered and documented before outcome fitting, in `TEMPORAL_DATE_AMENDMENT.md`; private hashed flags implement it.

The [2024 technical sheet, PDFpage3](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2024-5/FichaTecnica.pdf#page=3) explicitly establishes that matching`HV001` numbers across2023/2024 denote panel clusters. [PDFpage7](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2024-5/FichaTecnica.pdf#page=7) describes retention of two thirds of clusters while visiting different selected dwellings during a cluster's sample tenure. Thus this is a later-year repeated cross-section with shared contextual dependence, not within-person follow-up. Source PSU codes agree with individual/health PSU fields. All2,171shared source PSUs have a common reported UBIGEO, providing an additional consistency check, without using or exporting exact geographic coordinates.

Public annual household/person keys can recur despite the documented dwelling rotation. The two entire releases share2,279HHIDcodes and671CASEIDcodes;17same CASEIDcodes also have equal reported birth month/year. Among the two existing prepared cohorts,82CASEIDcodes recur and3also match birth month/year. These diagnostics are not verified personal identities and do not prove either duplication or complete person independence. Do not describe the full test as guaranteed person-independent or independent geography.

The fixed stricter sensitivity removes every2024PSU present in **all2023 released household records**, not merely the analytic training cohort. It retains3,250women in1,025PSUs,210strata and25regions, including45any-justification endorsers. This subset excludes all retained source clusters by a source-documented stable identity rule. It is a selected contextual subset, not a new nationally representative sample. Identifying a person's movement between different PSUs is beyond the available released keys.

## Weights, supported target and readiness

The [technical sheet, PDFpage11](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2024-5/FichaTecnica.pdf#page=11) distinguishes household`HV005`, women's`V005` and selected-health-person`PESO15_AMAS` weights and their respective PSU/stratum variables. These weights do not provide a documented joint inclusion probability for complete selected DV–health respondents. There is no additional weight in the actual DV CSV. Neither multiplying the two weights nor using one as a proven joint-module weight is justified by the reviewed documentation.

The supported primary target is therefore **equal-person prediction among observed eligible complete linked women**. The prepared2024 cohort contains9,847women in3,072PSUs,239strata and25regions, with positive released module weights retained privately for provenance. No national prevalence, national model performance or population effect is inferred. Conditional PSU-resampling intervals must remain explicitly conditional on these observed test respondents and fixed training models.

`MODEL_READY.json`, `READINESS.json`, source/category/date/overlap inventories and independent preparation QC bind the exact source decisions and files. A separate frozen evaluation protocol is required before fitting: train only eligible actual2023 respondents; retain the original2023 fixed models and feature blocks; test all eligible actual2024 respondents plus the predetermined new-PSU subset; prohibit later-outcome tuning. Sparse attitude endorsement requires its actual counts and uncertainty to accompany any incremental-prediction interpretation. A weak gain is not equivalence, absence of psychological relevance or proof that violence is unimportant.
