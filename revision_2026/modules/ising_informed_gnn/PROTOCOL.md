# Ising-informed item graph neural network: frozen exploratory design

22 October 2026. This analysis follows the completed ENDES Ising study. Existing outcomes and model comparisons have been examined. The present architecture and comparisons are retrospective exploratory tests, not prospective confirmation or preregistration. No existing manuscript, supplement, final export or earlier analysis is changed.

## Question and target

Does a fixed small graph neural network, informed by training-only pairwise justification associations, improve prediction of one missing questionnaire response after the other four answers are observed? This is a partial-questionnaire prediction task. It is not prediction before administering the questionnaire, prediction of future violence, a social network, a neural pathway, a causal attitude network or an education effect.

Use exactly the prior broader ENDES complete-response cohort: 29,102 adult women, 433 endorsing any item, five binary V744A–E responses, 3,249 observed PSUs and 240 released strata. Reuse the independently checked source hash and three whole-PSU partitions (42,43,44), with identical fivefold training memberships. The 2023 release includes 34 actual-2024 interviews; this is not a temporal experiment. The documented women's weight supports released-weighted complete-response losses; an equal-person analysis refits every new model. Neither weighting creates a national variance estimate.

## Masking and source isolation

For each woman create five prediction tasks, each hiding one target item. Set that target response to zero and its observed mask to zero BEFORE creating any feature or message. Other responses are known inputs. Node inputs are the observed response, observed mask and five fixed item-identity indicators. Each target's two possible hidden labels have exactly identical input tensors and graph. Women and their five tasks remain in the same entire-PSU fold. The 80 distinct masked input configurations are enumerated without inspecting frequencies; training uses their exact aggregated positive and negative weighted masses. Zero-mass configurations contribute zero loss. Configuration aggregation is computational only; held-out women remain the scoring and PSU-resampling units.

Reuse the matching prior training-only Ising state only after binding its source, exact fold membership and 32 training-pattern masses. Never use the prior full-cohort descriptive graph. Edges are the symmetric off-diagonal pair coefficients J from that training state, retaining their signs. Normalize each row by its sum of absolute coefficients; diagonal remains zero. Stop before fitting if a row has zero absolute sum or normalization is invalid. Report the verified minimum interaction across all matching training states. This graph is shared by respondents within a training fold and contains no test responses. Training answers legitimately estimate pooled associations; a held-out woman's hidden answer cannot affect graph construction.

## Fixed neural comparison

Let X contain the five nodes and seven features. Use H=ReLU([X,AX]W1+b1), and node logits=[H,AH]W2+b2 plus the corresponding training marginal-item logit offset. Eight hidden units give 137 trainable parameters. Offsets use only matching normalized training pattern masses, with probabilities clipped at 1e-6 for numerical protection. This is a two-stage mean message-passing model; the graph is fixed, while neural weights are learned. Fixed full-batch float64 Adam runs 400 updates at learning rate .03. Objective is the mean weighted masked Bernoulli negative log likelihood across all five item tasks plus 1/(2*N_train) times squared W1 and W2, a fixed unit-SD Gaussian weight prior on the per-woman average-conditional-risk scale; biases are unpenalized. The original .01 penalty proposal was corrected before any fitting because its scale could dominate rare-response likelihood; the original proposal/freeze are preserved in pre_fit_objective_history/ and the amendment is explicit. No response balancing, class weights, early stopping, hyperparameter search or held-out model choice occurs. Save all objective traces and states. Fixed-step training does not certify convergence.

Use three matched graph arms: real Ising adjacency; adjacency with fixed node-label permutation [2,4,1,0,3] while feature/item identities stay in their original positions; and identity adjacency (self-message). The real/permuted arms have identical weighted graph topology up to relabelling; their comparison tests alignment to item identities, not whether a graph exists. The self arm's missing node cannot access the other answers; it is an information-restricted control and its deficit cannot establish a topology advantage. Add an information-matched ordinary multilayer perceptron (MLP): flattened five masked responses, five observed masks and five target-item indicators, eight ReLU units, one output plus the same training marginal offset. This also has exactly 137 trainable parameters and sees all four known answers. Two initialization seeds42/43 are used in every arm; average their probabilities before scoring the primary neural ensemble. Identical settings, weights, offsets and participants apply to all arms. There are 3 partitions x5 folds x2 weightings x4 arms x2 initializations =240 neural fits.

## Simpler references

Score exact conditional probabilities from the matching previously fitted independent-item, common-acceptance and Ising joint distributions. These probabilities are computed by pairing each of the 32 states with its one-bit flip. Also fit five fixed conditional logistic regressions (C=1, L2, unpenalized intercept) using the other four responses only, one equation per missing item, training weights normalized to mean1, max_iter2000 and tol1e-10. Fit from aggregated response configurations using native binary predictors and positive/negative counts. This produces 150 logistic equations, not 150 independent populations. Asymmetric logistic equations and GNN conditionals need not define a compatible joint distribution; do not calculate or claim their whole-pattern likelihood.

## Scoring and uncertainty

Primary loss is released-weighted conditional-item Brier averaged over the five hidden-item tasks per woman, partition42. Prespecified graph contrasts are real minus exact Ising, common acceptance, conditional logistic, permuted graph and self-message. Lower is better. Report all contrasts, both weighting refits, both alternate partitions and the two initialization losses, including unfavorable results. Secondary loss is conditional-item negative log likelihood. Report item-specific losses and descriptive endorser-only loss, clearly conditional on an outcome-defined subset and not a separate prediction population. Because endorsement is rare, low absolute losses do not establish practical positive-case identification.

Use 2,000 paired resamples of observed PSUs within released strata, keeping the 18 singleton strata fixed, and reweight the denominator in every draw. The main uncertainty summaries condition on fixed training models/graphs/predictions and observed complete respondents. They omit retraining and singleton-stratum national variance, are not national confidence intervals, and do not test causal effects. The three partitions reuse the same women and are sensitivity analyses, not independent replications.
