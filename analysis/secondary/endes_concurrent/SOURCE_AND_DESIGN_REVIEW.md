# ENDES 2023 eligibility, coding and prediction target

20 October 2026. Preparation/source review only. The earlier readiness workspace and archive remain unchanged. The newly prepared respondent table and `MODEL_READY.json` are frozen for the separately owned prediction protocol; this review reports no fitted associations or model performance.

**The available data support equal-person prediction within a precisely defined observed respondent intersection. A validated national joint-module weight has not been established.** This distinction permits the authorized grouped prediction analysis while preventing an unsupported national prevalence, population effect or national prediction-performance claim.

## Selection and weights

The [official interviewer manual, p. 323](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2023-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=323), describes selection of one woman for the domestic-violence section using a “tabla de números aleatorios” on paper, with automatic tablet selection; privacy is a prerequisite. Household/tablet selection is also described on p. 128. The [same manual, p. 338](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2023-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=338), separately selects one person aged 15+ per household by the “cumpleaños más reciente ocurrido” method, with the lower household line resolving birthday ties. These are distinct module-selection procedures. The reviewed documentation does not establish their joint inclusion probabilities or justify treating their intersection as the entire selected-health female domain.

The [2023 annual-series methods, pp. 184–186](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/endes/2023/SERIES/SERIES_ANUALES_1986_2023.pdf#page=184), distinguish the women's weight `V005/1,000,000` (PSU `V001`, stratum `V022`) from the selected-health-person weight `PESO15_AMAS/1,000,000` (PSU `QHCLUSTER`, household stratum `HV022`). These weights address the corresponding released populations and nonresponse; neither is presented as a weight for the linked completed DV–attitude–health intersection. The actual 2023 DV CSV supplies no `D005`, `V005V` or other weight column. A special 2020 violence factor mentioned in older pandemic documentation was not assumed to exist in 2023.

Accordingly, preparation does not multiply weights, construct an invented adjustment or certify either released weight for national joint-module inference. The fixed primary target is **equal-person prediction error among the observed complete linked respondents satisfying the rules below**, with entire PSUs held out. The two released weights are retained privately as source fields; any future reweighted comparison would require its own explicitly labelled sensitivity protocol, and is not a validated national inference by default. The parent model protocol currently chooses equal-person prediction.

## Observed-intersection eligibility

The [official public release](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/sites/default/files/2023_2.zip), explicitly listed as Public/ODbL by the [national catalogue](https://www.datosabiertos.gob.pe/dataset/encuesta-demogr%C3%A1fica-y-de-salud-familiar-endes-2023-instituto-nacional-de-estad%C3%ADstica-e), supplies the actual CSV modules and their dictionaries. Preparation requires, cumulatively:

1. Individual age 18–49 and completed individual interview (`V015=1`). This adult boundary differs from the original aggregate 15–49 analysis.
2. A unique household/person-line link to the selected health respondent, recorded as female and aged 18–49; completed selected-person interview (`QSRESINF=1`) and completed health questionnaire (`QSRESULT=1`). Requiring both completion flags defines a conservative complete-intersection target. It does not silently equate the two fields.
3. DV selection and interview (`V044=1`); code2 identifies privacy impossible and code3 selection without interview, so those are excluded. The interviewing design specifies direct selected-person interviews. An additional per-answer proxy-status field was not available or inferred.
4. Currently or formerly married/cohabiting (`V502` in 1,2). This is an **ever-married/cohabiting** restriction; it does not include every never-married woman who has had a dating partner, even though the contemporary DV questionnaire can ask about dating relationships. Native marital categories remain in the baseline predictors.
5. All nine 14-day depression item codes 0–3; all five attitude items coded binary0/1 (unknown8 excluded); all requested observed emotional, physical and sexual item codes0–3; valid native baseline categories; source PSU/stratum fields available.

`cohort_flow.csv` reports each exclusion stage. The frozen table contains **10,267 respondents across 3,116 PSUs, 239 strata and 25 departmental regions**. These counts define this conditional prediction cohort rather than a nationally representative joint sample. All retained records have positive released-health and women's weights, but positivity does not validate joint weighting. Household `HV022` agrees with individual `V022`, and the household, individual and health PSU fields agree record by record. Core and health ages agree. Independent source reconstruction verifies every eligible source key and 236,141 prepared covariate/item/score/exposure fields (`INDEPENDENT_PREPARATION_QC.json`).

## Outcome and IPV timeframes

The [manual, p. 375](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2023-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=375), explicitly identifies PHQ-9 and the “últimos 14 días” reference. The actual released `QS700A`–`QS700I` items are scored0–3 and summed to `phq9_total` (0–27). Their order differs from the conventional questionnaire presentation; summing all nine requires no reverse scoring. The recalled-episode `QS704*` block is not substituted. Symptom scores are not diagnoses, and none of these nine outcome items is an eligible predictor of its own total.

The [manual, p. 326](https://proyectos.inei.gob.pe/iinei/srienaho/Descarga/DocumentosMetodologicos/2023-5/ManualEntrevistadora.pdf#page=326), first asks whether each act ever occurred, then the “últimos 12 meses” frequency among positive histories. The released dictionaries encode0 as a negative lifetime history,1 as frequent recent occurrence,2 as occasional recent occurrence and3 as a positive lifetime history with no occurrence in the recent reference period. Therefore recent exposure is any1/2; lifetime exposure is any1/2/3. A code3 must not become a negative lifetime history. All required source item responses must be observed before either derived exposure is assigned.

- **Emotional acts:** `D103A` humiliation, `D103B` threatened harm, `D103D` threatened departure/removal of children or economic support. `D103C` is entirely blank in this release and is excluded from the definition rather than imputed. These three acts do not exhaust all controlling, psychological or economic abuse in the newer questionnaire. Their mapped three-item definition is explicit throughout.
- **Physical acts:** `D105A`–`D105G`, the seven documented acts covering pushing/shaking/throwing something; slapping/arm twisting; punching/hitting; kicking/dragging; attempted strangling/burning; weapon threats; and weapon attacks. The generic additional `D105J` and country-specific placeholders were not assumed equivalent or filled with zero.
- **Sexual acts:** `D105H` forced intercourse and `D105I` forced other sexual acts.

Primary exposure columns use the recent timeframe. Lifetime columns are retained for source verification and a separately documented potential sensitivity, not silently substituted. Neither cross-sectional source timing nor holding out respondents establishes that norms caused violence or depressive symptoms.

## Native baseline categories and unavailable religion

The actual module dictionaries are retained with source hashes in `docs/`; they can be reacquired from the official release archive. Baseline inputs are continuous age (`V012`); education (`V106`: no education/primary/secondary/higher); relative wealth quintile (`V190`:1–5); current work (`V714`:0/1); marital status (`V501`:1married,2cohabiting,3widowed,4divorced,5not living together); residence (`V025`:1urban,2rural); and departmental region (`V024`:1–25). All categorical codes remain native. Employment is current work; the separate past-year `V731` field is retained with its original categories and is not collapsed into current employment or harmonised to another dataset.

Religion `V130` is entirely blank in the actual release. It cannot be evaluated for this cohort; the limitation is recorded rather than inventing a category or an adjustment. `category_inventory.csv` and `missingness.csv` preserve unknowns, blank fields and unmatched-module cases in aggregate form. All identifiers, raw item rows and exclusion flags remain exclusively inside `data_local/`.

The separately owned prediction protocol must fit identical cohorts for nested comparisons, group holdout by PSU and perform all scaling/one-hot fitting on training data. Added attitudes test incremental prediction beyond measured covariates/IPV, not an individual causal mechanism. No novelty claim is certified by this preparation review.
