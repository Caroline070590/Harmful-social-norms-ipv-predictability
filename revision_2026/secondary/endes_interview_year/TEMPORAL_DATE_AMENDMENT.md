# Actual interview dates take precedence over release labels

20 October 2026. Saved before any temporal outcome fitting. The independent source review and producer readiness audit found that the frozen ENDES2023 release is not entirely composed of calendar2023 interviews: its existing prepared intersection includes eight women with both individual and selected-health interviews in2024. The other10,259 prepared women have both interviews in2023. The older release-based analysis remains unchanged and retains its original cohort definition.

For this new strictly ordered temporal comparison, training is restricted to records whose **actual individual interview year and actual selected-health interview year both equal2023**. The eight2024 records are excluded from training before fits. All9,847 prepared later-test records have both interviews in2024. These decisions use source dates, not symptom values, associations, predictions or model performance. Frozen cohort files are preserved; separate hashed training/test eligibility flags record the new design.

The2024 technical sheet explicitly establishes that matching `HV001` values identify panel clusters across2023/2024 (PDFpage3), while selected dwellings differ during a cluster's tenure (PDFpage7). This supports a later-year repeated-cross-section test with possible shared contextual dependence. A fixed secondary test retains only2024 PSUs absent from **all2023 released household PSUs**, which is stricter than absence from the smaller analytic training sample. Public annual household/person keys are also audited, but key equality or inequality alone does not verify personal identity. Neither comparison is described as within-person follow-up or prospective confirmation.
