# ENDES later-year replication: source and preparation protocol

20 October 2026. Written before examining the later-year symptom distributions or fitting models. This workspace preserves the completed ENDES 2023 preparation, models, manuscripts and packages. No outcome model is authorized by this preparation document alone.

The proposed extension tests whether the existing fixed 2023 attitude/IPV/demographic prediction comparisons reproduce in genuinely later respondents. It concerns concurrent PHQ-9 symptoms measured in the later year, not forecasting a person's later symptoms from their 2023 interview. A filename ending in 2024 does not prove later interview dates, independent people, or independent PSUs.

1. Retrieve only the official public ODbL INEI release and documentation. Keep archive and individual rows exclusively in excluded `data_local/`. Save exact URLs, HTTP status, retrieval time and hashes. Do not acquire restricted records or circumvent authentication.
2. Verify later-year questionnaire/dictionary/manual definitions against the previously fixed 2023 sources. Required blocks: five binary `V744A`–`V744E` justification items; nine actual 14-day `QS700A`–`QS700I` PHQ-9 items; three observed emotional acts `D103A/B/D`, seven physical acts `D105A`–`D105G` and two sexual acts `D105H/I`. Do not assume the same field name establishes equivalence. Preserve code3 as lifetime but not recent exposure. Missing/unknowns are not zero. Religion and unused `D103C` availability must be rechecked.
3. Repeat the fixed 2023 complete-intersection eligibility: women18–49, completed individual and selected-health interviews, DV selection/interview/privacy, ever married/cohabiting, verified complete source-coded items and native baseline categories. Verify unique same-person linkage with household and line number, key/PSU/stratum/age consistency and category/missingness inventories. Save a sequential exclusion ledger.
4. Audit actual individual and selected-health interview years and months. Tabulate release-year versus interview-year combinations. A later-year test must contain both individual and health interviews strictly after 2023. Earlier-year records in an annual health release are excluded explicitly. Test/training row identities must never be inferred merely by year-prefixed IDs.
5. Audit source documentation for rolling samples, revisits and cluster/household rotation. Compare raw household/person keys across annual releases with explicit warning that annual identifier reuse can produce false apparent overlap. Where stable geographic/PSU/household fields support shared-cluster identification, export aggregate overlap counts. If true respondent independence cannot be established, describe the limitation and consider an explicitly conservative no-shared-identifier sensitivity, without calling it verified person deduplication.
6. Reassess joint DV/health selection and weights from official 2024 documentation. The prior2023 weight limitation persists unless a valid new design argument establishes a joint-intersection weight. Never multiply or substitute module weights. The supported target, if measurement/date gates pass, is equal-person prediction among the observed complete linked respondents, with design-aware grouped uncertainty conditional on this cohort. Do not claim national prevalence, clinical diagnosis, causal effects or pandemic effects.
7. Freeze source/definition decisions, preparation code and cohort hash before any later outcome fit. Write machine-readable readiness including independent-person and national-weight gates. Stop unsupported computation cleanly and write no placeholder numerical findings. A separate fixed evaluation protocol must precede fitting and prohibit tuning on later outcomes.

Prepared outcomes may be constructed to establish complete source linkage and scoring validity; no correlations, exposure effects or performance estimates are generated in this readiness phase.
