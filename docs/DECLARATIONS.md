# Declarations

## Ethics approval and consent to participate

This study is a secondary analysis of published aggregate estimates and de-identified public survey releases. No participants were recruited or contacted for this analysis. Ethics and consent arrangements of the original studies are described in the providers’ documentation. No separate ethics approval or waiver was obtained for this secondary analysis, and no formal exemption determination is claimed.

## Consent for publication

Not applicable. No identifiable individual information is presented, and this repository contains no respondent records.

## Author contributions

The author conceived the study, performed the analysis, interpreted the results and wrote the manuscript. Computational and editorial AI assistance is disclosed in the manuscript; scientific verification and approval remain the author’s responsibility.
