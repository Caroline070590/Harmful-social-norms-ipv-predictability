# ENDES public respondent prediction: preparation protocol

20 October 2026. Saved before preparation of this new analysis cohort; earlier readiness records were inspected and remain frozen. No new association or prediction estimates are produced by preparation.

Primary target for the authorized subsequent analysis: incremental held-out prediction of 14-day depressive symptoms from the five individual justification responses beyond native demographic/socioeconomic predictors and measured emotional/physical/sexual IPV. Retain the original attitude paper as primary. Use adults aged 18–49; keep this boundary distinct from the earlier aggregate 15–49 targets. Religion is absent in every released 2023 individual record and cannot be modelled.

Preparation must verify official module eligibility, interview/privacy status, partnership universe, native category codes, the nine `QS700*` depression response codes and the actual current IPV item mappings/recall periods. Preserve unknowns and missing values. Emotional `D103C` is unused; observed A/B/D require a documented recent/lifetime definition. Only deterministic source-documented harmonisation is allowed. Retain duplicate/unmatched/key and exclusion ledgers.

Inspect sampling/manual documentation before selecting weights. Do not multiply module weights or invent a violence-selection adjustment. If no validated joint-module population weight can be established, report that gate and identify the narrower observed complete-intersection prediction target that can legitimately be tested with grouped holdout, without national prevalence or population-effect claims. Do not represent a released module weight as a proven joint-sample weight.

Save respondent-level preparation only in this stage's `data_local/`; export aggregate source/category/missingness/eligibility/design inventories and code/package hashes. Existing readiness sources, results, protocols, QC and archives must remain unchanged. All model fitting and evaluation will be performed in a subsequent fixed protocol, not by preparation.
