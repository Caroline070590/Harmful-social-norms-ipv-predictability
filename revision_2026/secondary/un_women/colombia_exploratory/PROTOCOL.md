# Exploratory UN Women pilot: reports of worsening and individual symptom associations

19 October 2026, saved before fitting. Author confirmed both questions and women as the primary population. This is a post-primary exploratory extension using a newly inspected public release; not preregistration, causal identification, replication independent of this source's published report, or a replacement for the original attitude study. Country counts, direct-question response frequencies and broad-mental-impact frequencies have already been inspected. No symptom association model has been fitted.

Question 1: estimate country-specific weighted proportions reporting increased local violence/abuse/harassment (`C08`), plus separately labelled vignette worsening (`C11`), household conflict increase (`C20`) and home safety worsening (`C22`). Keep different meanings and conditional denominators explicit. For Colombia only, describe increased frequency (`C26_2`) among women answering that conditional direct-IPV change question. These are retrospective reports/perceptions, not observed incidence changes, national clinical prevalence or pandemic causal effects. Do not claim there was an increase regardless of results.

Question 2: in Colombia, compare women reporting any direct partner violence (original `C26` = often/a few times/rarely) with never. Exclude not-applicable, refused, don't-know and structurally missing records explicitly. Retain the original frequencies rather than the top-two grouped version, which combines rarely with never. Do not classify other countries' structural absence of the question as no IPV.

Two primary exploratory continuous outcomes: (i) the sum of the Phase II anhedonia and depressed-mood items, each recoded 0–3 (sum 0–6); (ii) the Phase II uncontrollable-worry item (0–3). Phase II uses a since-COVID reference period, not the standard two-week PHQ/GAD recall period. Therefore call these a depressive-symptom item sum and a worry item, not validated PHQ-2/GAD-2 scales, diagnostic thresholds, or clinical diseases. Do not pool with Phase I, which asks three different stress questions on a five-point, last-month scale. Do not calculate a GAD-2 score from one worry item.

Use one fixed survey-weighted linear association model per primary outcome, adjusting age category, original education category, urban/rural locality and marital category. Main effects only; no automated model selection or interactions. Use the same complete symptom/covariate cohort for both outcomes where possible, logging exclusions. Holm-adjust across the two direct-IPV coefficients. Show unadjusted weighted group means and adjusted differences with uncertainty. No cut-point search, neural embedding, or predictive superiority claim is made.

Secondary broad mental-impact outcome: report weighted proportions indicating any negative impact (negative or both negative/positive), by direct-IPV group. This is descriptive and separate from the symptom models, not an additional primary significance test.

Use supplied country weights, verify finite positive values and build the design on full released records before subsetting country/eligible domains. No PSU/strata fields are released. For this pilot, `survey` with independent respondent IDs and supplied weights gives approximate Taylor-linearized uncertainty under an independent-respondent assumption; it does not recover the full original sampling/nonresponse uncertainty. Document this limitation prominently; use t intervals and do not claim exact national design-based inference. Country-specific descriptive intervals are pointwise, not discoveries adjusted across countries. No pooled global estimate is planned.

Save raw input hash, reproducible code, coding/eligibility ledgers, counts, source question crosswalk, weighted descriptions, two full model coefficient tables, environment and QC. Keep respondent records local and out of archives. Verify country/ID uniqueness, original symptom coding, approximate intervals, and weighted means independently. Cases with before-only timing despite a last-12-month direct question must be flagged; do not reinterpret their histories as incident post-COVID violence. This timing issue and the cross-sectional design prevent conclusions about violence preceding symptoms or its causal effect.

Keep all pre-existing GFS, aggregate and inconclusive results. The pilot's limitations determine main/supplement allocation later; the manuscript is not rewritten during this pilot.
