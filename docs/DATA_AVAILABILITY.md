# Data and code availability

Analysis code, official aggregate inputs, public derived summaries and reproduction commands for *Structural context and women’s endorsement of justifications for intimate partner violence* are available in this repository: <https://github.com/Caroline070590/Harmful-social-norms-ipv-predictability>. See [ANALYSES.md](ANALYSES.md) for the reproduction scope of each analysis; reproduction of aggregate predictions and summaries is distinguished from refitting models from original source records.

The national analysis uses official estimates and survey metadata from the [DHS Program API](https://api.dhsprogram.com/) and documented external MICS-family survey reports. Structural indicators come from the UNDP Human Development Report data and V-Dem indices distributed by Our World in Data. Exact source releases, retrieval information and model inputs are identified in `analysis/central/` and the module documentation.

The historical aggregate compilation *Violence Against Women & Girls* (Kaggle) was used as a benchmark and compared with official estimates. It contains published group percentages, not respondent records, and is not redistributed in this repository; it can be obtained from its provider.

Separate individual-level analyses use releases from INEI (ENDES), Afrobarometer, the Global Flourishing Study, COVIDiSTRESS and UN Women under their respective access and reuse conditions. This repository contains no respondent records or fitted respondent-level model states; refitting those analyses requires the original provider files. Participants are not linked across studies or to the national aggregate estimates.
