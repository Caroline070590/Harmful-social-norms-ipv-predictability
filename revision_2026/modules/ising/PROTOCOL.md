# Exact finite-state justification network: frozen exploratory protocol

22 October 2026. This new analysis does not change existing results, manuscript or independent final export. Source/joint-pattern feasibility has been inspected; no outcome model has been fitted. The five complete binary attitude items in the previously audited broader ENDES adult women's cohort define the target. The 2023 release contains 34 actual-2024 records, explicitly reported; this is not a temporal experiment.

## Question and estimand

Do pairwise conditional associations among five justification responses improve held-out whole-pattern prediction beyond independent items and a single common acceptance tendency? Each graph node is a questionnaire item, not a woman, country or brain region. Interaction coefficients are conditional response associations, not influence, contagion, causal effects, anatomical connections or measured temporal transitions. No centrality or country-transfer claim is planned.

## Exact models

Enumerate all 32 binary patterns in V744A–E order, least-significant bit first. Independent model has five item intercepts. The Ising model has five intercepts and all ten pair interactions: log P(y)=sum h_j y_j + sum J_jk y_j y_k - log Z, with Z computed exactly over 32 states. Fit exact weighted joint likelihood, not pseudolikelihood. A fixed N(0,5^2) intercept penalty and N(0,1^2) interaction penalty stabilize rare/empty cells. No edge selection, tuning grid, significance threshold or post-outcome architecture search occurs.

The prespecified competing common-acceptance model is a one-factor random-intercept Rasch model: conditionally independent logistic items h_j+sigma*z, z~N(0,1), integrated with 61-point Gauss–Hermite quadrature. It has five item intercepts, one nonnegative common scale and the same intercept prior, with fixed half-normal scale2 penalty; bounds sigma0.001–10 and fixed initial scales0.5/1.5/3 are numerical safeguards, not model selection by test performance. The best training objective is retained. This baseline tests whether one shared endorsement propensity suffices; residual Ising edges cannot prove a network-generating mechanism.

## Validation and primary scoring

Use the prior outcome-blind, whole-PSU fivefold assignment algorithm with fixed seeds42,43,44. Seed42 is primary; the other two partitions are stability sensitivities, not independent cohorts. Training weights are normalized to sum to the training sample size. The primary fit/loss uses the documented women's weight V005/1,000,000; a complete equal-person refit is a fixed sensitivity. Every model/weighting/partition/fold uses the identical women and held-out PSUs; no model parameter is learned from held-out responses. Exactly90 held-out fits (3 models x2 weightings x3 partitions x5 folds) and six full-cohort descriptive fits are planned. Training sufficient statistics, normalized masses, parameters and 32-state probabilities are retained locally.

Primary held-out loss is joint-pattern negative log likelihood per woman, paired Ising-minus-common and Ising-minus-independent contrasts (lower is better). Report the 32-category Brier score, unconditional item Brier and its exact mean/centered-profile decomposition. Also report conditional-item Brier/log loss from P(item | other four responses); these measure missing-response prediction when the other responses are already known, not prediction before administering the questionnaire. Sparse endorsement and rejection-dominated averages remain explicit; no classification threshold is tuned. Endorser-only descriptive scores may be shown but are not a separate population or primary loss.

For uncertainty, 2,000 fixed-prediction resamples of observed PSUs within released strata give conditional paired loss intervals. The18 singleton strata remain fixed; no national design interval, national pvalue or full-model-retraining prediction uncertainty is claimed. Separate200 stratified whole-PSU refits of the primary weighted full Ising model describe edge sign/magnitude stability in this observed complete-response cohort; singleton PSUs again remain fixed. Bootstrap edge percentile ranges are sensitivity summaries, not calibrated national edge CIs. Report all ten edges, no claimed stable ranking. Nonconvergent fits are disclosed and not silently dropped.
